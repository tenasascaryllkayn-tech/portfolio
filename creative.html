🔍 The Work

What print-ready creative design actually involves.

Design that's meant to be printed has rules a screen design doesn't: correct color mode (CMYK), correct resolution (300 DPI), safe margins for trimming, and materials that hold up in real hands.

I create nature-inspired stickers, layouts, and warm visual assets for teachers, small brands, and personal projects — with files that arrive ready to send straight to the printer.
